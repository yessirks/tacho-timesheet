Everything (details, signature, presets, places, diary, your edits and expenses) is kept in this browser's storage on this device, like cookies but bigger. Download a copy now and then, and use it to move everything to your phone or a new computer. On iPhone, add the app to your Home Screen, otherwise Safari may clear it after a few weeks unused.
